# Design direction

Avoid unnecessary secondary labels and descriptions. Let the layout do the explaining.

- Preserve the light background, orange accent, generous spacing, and connected module layout.
- Preserve the overview composition: Perception at upper left, Memory at upper right, System slightly lower between them, and Navigation directly below System. Do not flatten the three upper cards into a centered row.
- Keep the hardware photo at its natural aspect ratio and vertically centered beside the sensor list; its frame must not stretch into an empty panel.
- Judge labels by their purpose. Keep object names, robot/path labels, axes, legends, and other annotations that help explain a figure; make them readable. Avoid blanket removal of small text.
- Omit decorative microcopy, fake coordinate readouts, serial numbers, redundant section labels, and instructions that repeat what links or layout already communicate.
- Keep useful research descriptions, module interfaces, contributor names, paper links, and video availability clear and readable.
- Use roughly 16px for body copy and at least 14px for supporting text, with sufficient contrast. Preserve readability on phones rather than shrinking the type.
- Overview videos and main module detail videos autoplay silently, loop inline, and open in the existing enlarged dialog when clicked. Keep a usable pause control. Video galleries open recordings on demand.
- Perception focuses on the sensing pipeline and hardware; omit its paper section. Navigation hosts the PSG-Nav video gallery.
- Use the same compact paper rows on the overview and module pages: thumbnail, linked title, short summary, author/arXiv metadata, and available code/project links. Preserve readable type; do not restore large paper feature cards.
- All three animated connections run from the module toward System, including Navigation.
